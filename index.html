Profile

I am a Luxury Brand Management postgraduate at the University of Southampton, with a foundation in Business Sciences from Forman Christian College, Lahore. My academic and professional trajectory has been shaped by a deep interest in how luxury brands cultivate meaning, desire, and loyalty across cultures.

"Luxury is not about wealth — it is about meaning, narrative, and the art of making the ordinary extraordinary."

My research explores the tension between brand consistency and cultural adaptation, particularly in the rapidly growing GCC luxury market. Practically, I have advised on CRM strategy, led market research for international expansion, and developed brand consultancy proposals for British luxury watchmaker Bremont and the House of Fabergé.

I am seeking an internship or entry-level role in luxury fashion, retail, or brand marketing where I can apply strategic insight and cultural intelligence in a client-facing environment.
